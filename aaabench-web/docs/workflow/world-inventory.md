# The world inventory

The catalogue of KINDS of thing a real map contains. Not a shopping list — a check against the
short list everyone builds from memory (buildings, roads, cars, trees, lamps) which is why cities
built from memory look empty.

Each group ends with *the rule*: where the thing goes, because placement is what makes these read
as systems rather than scatter.

## Roads and their furniture

carriageway · kerb · gutter · drain · manhole · service cover · pavement · tactile paving ·
crossing · refuge island · median · roundabout · slip road · bus lane · cycle lane · parking bay ·
loading bay · disabled bay · yellow lines · give-way markings · lane arrows · speed hump ·
bollard · barrier · guard rail · crash barrier · road sign · street name sign · traffic signal ·
pedestrian signal · pole · junction box · street lamp · litter bin · bench · bus shelter · timetable ·
shelter advertising · cycle rack · phone cabinet · post box · parking meter · CCTV pole · gritting bin

*Rule:* furniture aligns to the kerb line and clusters where it is used — a bin at a bench, a rack
at a shop, a meter every 6 parking bays. Nothing free-floats in the middle of a pavement.

## Utilities and infrastructure

substation · transformer · pylon · pole-mounted transformer · overhead line · cable duct ·
inspection chamber · water tower · pumping station · water treatment works · sewage works ·
gas governor · district heating pipe · telecoms cabinet · mast · dish array · fibre chamber ·
weather station · fire hydrant · dry riser · standpipe · vent shaft · culvert · flood gate ·
sea wall · groyne · retaining wall · noise barrier

*Rule:* infrastructure is where the land was cheap and the route was direct. It is fenced, it is
signed, it has an access track, and it is never in the nice district unless it was there first.

*Rule:* transport is a network with continuity. A rail line that stops, a road that ends in

## Industry and work

warehouse · distribution shed · loading dock · yard · silo · hopper · conveyor · chimney ·
cooling tower · tank farm · gasometer · scrap yard · crusher · quarry face · spoil heap ·
cement plant · saw mill · fish processing · cold store · abattoir · brewery · workshop · foundry ·
boat yard · net store · ice plant · fuel depot · builder's merchant · plant hire · skip yard ·
recycling centre · landfill · incinerator

*Rule:* industry follows the rail, the water and the road out. It is downwind and downstream of
the money, and it has a shift pattern you can see: lorries in the morning, empty at 2 p.m.,
security light at night.

## Commerce and the street

corner shop · supermarket · market stall · covered market · butcher · baker · greengrocer ·
fishmonger · off-licence · takeaway · café · restaurant · pub · bar · club · hotel · hostel ·
barber · salon · nail bar · laundrette · dry cleaner · tailor · cobbler · locksmith · hardware ·
electrical · phone repair · pawnbroker · betting shop · charity shop · bank · ATM · bureau ·
estate agent · solicitor · accountant · dentist · vet · gym · tattoo · tanning · pet shop ·
bookshop · record shop · toy shop · furniture · carpet · tiles · bathroom showroom · garden centre

*Rule:* retail rents sort themselves along the street. Banks and chains at the crossing, services
mid-block, the pawnbroker and the takeaway at the cheap end. Vacancy is real: board up 5–15 % of
units and more at the poor end, with a ghost sign above.

*Rule:* managed nature is in a line and the same age; unmanaged nature is where nobody goes —
railway edges, waste ground, the gap between the fence and the wall.

## How to use this

Pick a block you have already built. Walk the list. Count how many groups are represented. A first
attempt usually has roads, buildings, lamps, bins and trees — five groups out of nine, and the
missing four are exactly the ones that say *people work here, and someone maintains this*.
